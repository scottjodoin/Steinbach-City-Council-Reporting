Every decision and debate on business growth

What has Steinbach City Council decided, debated and promised about business growth and investment since December 2015, and how did each item end?

In short

Each row is one decision, debate, promise or request on the topic, from the minutes and the meeting videos. Filter by term, year, theme, council member or result. “No public result” means we found no public decision or follow-up; some work happens in closed study sessions and committees.

2015–16Mobile sign permits extended from 30 days to a full year (By-law 2066)After businesses complained when enforcement of the 2015 zoning by-law began, council amended the mobile sign rules so a business can get an annual (365-day) permit instead of 30-day permits with 30-day gaps and a 90-day yearly cap. Councillors Siemens and a Councillor Penner opposed first reading, arguing it makes temporary signs permanent and ignores the earlier public consultation on the city's appearance.Done First reading carried Dec 15, 2015 with Siemens and one Councillor Penner speaking against; second and third reading carried Jan 19, 2016 (no objections at the public hearing) and took effect immediately. Council kept the 365-day permit when it revisited the rules in Sept 2016.By-law 2066 · vote: First reading carried with opposition (Siemens and one Councillor Penner spoke against); second and third reading carried, opponents not named
