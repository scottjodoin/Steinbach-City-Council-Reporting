Every decision and debate on services in new and fringe neighbourhoods

What has Steinbach City Council decided, debated and promised about services in new and fringe neighbourhoods since December 2015, and how did each item end?

In short

Each row is one decision, debate, promise or request on the topic, from the minutes and the meeting videos. Filter by term, year, theme, council member or result. “No public result” means we found no public decision or follow-up; some work happens in closed study sessions and committees.

2015–16Barkman Avenue rebuilt as a local improvement (Main to Henry Street)Council used a local improvement plan (LIP 2016-01, By-law 2060) to rebuild about three blocks of Barkman Avenue, one of Steinbach's older streets: surface works, water main and sewer main. About half was borrowed over five years and most of the rest came from federal and provincial grants. One objection was registered. Council saw it as part of a planned, long-term renewal of older streets.Done First and second reading Dec 15, 2015. The Municipal Board approved the borrowing and third reading was given Feb 16, 2016. Construction was part of the 2016 capital works tender ($2.955M to Maple Leaf Construction, Mar 15, 2016).By-law 2060 (LIP 2016-01) · vote: carried; no opposition recorded
2015–17Home Street North drainage swale: residents' petition; fix judged too costlyResidents of Home Street North asked council to replace an open drainage swale that sends water and ice across the street (an area the city took over from Hanover decades ago). Council asked for a report in Dec 2015 and, after a delegation and petition in Feb 2017, passed a motion for costed options. In Dec 2017 the City Manager reported that a culvert or bridge crossing would cost about $500,000 plus property purchases, so the city would only monitor and maintain the crossing until the street is someday rebuilt.Declined Dec 5, 2017: no reconstruction; extra monitoring and maintenance continue; reconfiguration only 'in the relatively distant future' when the street is due for reconstruction. The Mayor asked that the residents be told the cost.vote: carried (Feb 7, 2017 motion for a costed options report); Dec 2017 report received, no action
