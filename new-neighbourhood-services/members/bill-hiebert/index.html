Services in new neighbourhoods · Council member

Bill Hiebert: services in new and fringe neighbourhoods

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about services in new subdivisions and fringe areas (grading, sidewalks, parks, paving, wells and septic) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert's part in this topic was mostly procedural. He seconded routine items such as street lights for new streets, capital works tenders, drain takeovers, subdivision approvals and the Clearspring Greens sewer reroute, and voted with the majority on every Clearspring Greens pathway decision, from the 80/20 plan to the 50/50 compromise.

His recorded differences were few. In 2023 he cast the lone vote against refusing a reduced setback on an annexed lot that staff said could limit drainage, and in 2025 he voted for the Sterling Crescent front-yard variance that the city engineer opposed. We found no high-confidence quote from him on this topic.

Term by term

2018–2022

Voted with the majority on the Clearspring Greens plans (80/20, the lighting change and the 50/50 compromise) and seconded routine tenders, street-light additions and subdivision approvals. No recorded dissent on this topic.

Took part in 9 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Seconded street-light, drain and tender items, including the Wyndham Estate Drive sidewalk program and the Clearspring Greens reroute. Cast the lone vote against denying the annexed-lot setback variance (2023) and voted for the Sterling Crescent variance (2025).

Took part in 21 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A quiet, consistent vote with the majority on services in new neighbourhoods, leaning toward allowing owners' variances.
