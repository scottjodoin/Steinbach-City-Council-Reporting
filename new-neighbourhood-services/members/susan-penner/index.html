Services in new neighbourhoods · Council member

Susan Penner: services in new and fringe neighbourhoods

What did Susan Penner say and do about services in new subdivisions and fringe areas (grading, sidewalks, parks, paving, wells and septic) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor in all three terms covered · Deputy Mayor from November 2026 (topped the 2026 poll)

In short

Susan Penner's main contribution on this topic was the 2021 Clearspring Greens compromise. After 78 objections sank the first plan, she proposed dropping the lights and splitting the path 50/50, with each lot's share capped at the $1,610 sidewalk fee 'that was not factored in the price of the lots of Clear Spring Greens.' It passed unanimously and the path was built. She also moved council's objections to Hanover allowing rural lots and below-standard businesses on the city's edge, asked for Bush Farm Road at a study session, pressed to finish paving Park Road West, and moved the Manning Canal takeover.

In 2023 she was the lone vote against refusing a stop sign by the new Hampton Village park, because children had no safe crossing. But in 2025 she defended not committing to sidewalks in Park Hill, saying bays and crescents commonly have none, and she voted for the Sterling Crescent variance. In 2015–2018 her remarks are hard to tell from Cari Penner's in the recordings.

“I would like council to consider doing a new plan for the sidewalk that would see the lights removed from the project and then splitting the cost of the asphalt path 50/50. … This is a fee that was not factored in the price of the lots of Clear Spring Greens when it was developed.”

Susan Penner, Councillor · Proposing a new Clearspring Greens pathway plan · Jan 5, 2021 · 0:35:38

Term by term

2014–2018

Few statements can be attributed to her, because two Councillors Penner served and the recordings often don't say which spoke. She moved the 2018 variance excusing a Park Road West business from paving four parking stalls and voted for the Harmony Lane reserve change.

Took part in 4 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

2018–2022

Moved the 2019 objection to rural lots on Hanover's side of the boundary and asked for Bush Farm Road at the July 2020 study session. Proposed and moved the Clearspring Greens 50/50 plan capped at $1,610, and noted that 60 owners prepaid. Defended the Acres Drive levy as based on land and backed phasing 'what makes sense, what's efficient.'

Took part in 13 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Pressed to finish paving Park Road West, voted alone for a safer crossing by the Hampton Village park, moved the objection to Hanover's fringe rezoning and the Manning Canal agreement, and defended not committing sidewalks in Park Hill's bays and crescents. Voted for the Sterling Crescent variance.

Took part in 18 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A problem-solver on cost-sharing who found the formula that ended the Clearspring Greens dispute, while generally accepting the city's sidewalks-later practice for new areas.
