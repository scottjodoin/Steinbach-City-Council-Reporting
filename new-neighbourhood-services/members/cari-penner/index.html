Services in new neighbourhoods · Council member

Cari Penner: services in new and fringe neighbourhoods

What did Cari Penner say and do about services in new subdivisions and fringe areas (grading, sidewalks, parks, paving, wells and septic) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner seconded the 2016 Steinbach North swale exception and argued for it: 'I feel that city specs need to be relaxed.' Two years later she took the opposite side on paving, as the only member to vote against letting a Park Road West business skip paving its parking (6–1), after voting with the mayor against the general gravel-road surfacing rule (4–2). She said paving had been agreed to in the original development.

She seconded the Clearwater Avenue sidewalk grant and the Harmony Lane public reserve change, which she voted for. Many 2016–2017 remarks by 'Councillor Penner' could be either her or Susan Penner and are not attributed.

“I feel that city specs need to be relaxed. I believe it's in the best interests of the city to relax our specs and allow this development to go forward.”

Councillor Cari Penner · Seconding the Steinbach North swale exception · May 3, 2016 · 0:21:25

Term by term

2014–2018

Seconded and argued for the 2016 swale exception; seconded the Clearwater Avenue sidewalk grant application (2017); voted against relaxing paving for businesses on gravel roads (4–2) and was the lone vote against the Park Road West paving variance (6–1, 2018), on the principle of honouring signed agreements; seconded and voted for the Harmony Lane reserve change (4–3).

Took part in 6 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

Willing to relax specifications for a new development, but firm that businesses should honour the paving they agreed to.
