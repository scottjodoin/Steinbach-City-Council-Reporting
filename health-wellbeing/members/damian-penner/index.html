Damian Penner: health care and healthy living

What did Damian Penner say and do about health care and healthy living in Steinbach (doctors and clinics, emergency services, mental health, seniors, recreation) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Damian Penner moved the 2019 pool fee increase to cut a deficit of over $800,000 a year, the first Clearspring Greens pathway by-law (a connector he said the neighbourhood should mostly pay for), and the creation of the Mayor's Doctor Recruitment Task Force in 2024.

He seconded the wellbeing plan and the silica sand water review, said he was glad the city 'stepped up' to fund STARS, highlighted walking and cycling links in the draft community plan, and moved the 2026 budget. His split votes were about land use: against a children's charity's book sales in the industrial park (2022) and against the apartment beside the hospital (2024).

“This is a budget for our kids, for the working families, and for seniors aging at home.”

Councillor Damian Penner · Moving the 2026 budget · Mar 17, 2026 · 0:38:22

Term by term

2018–2022

Moved the 2019 pool fee increase, the first Clearspring Greens by-law and a care-home visitation shelter; seconded the silica sand water review ('the most valuable resource that we have is clean drinking water'); moved the grants list that included STARS and the Bethesda Foundation.

Took part in 10 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Moved the doctor task force, seconded the wellbeing plan and the seniors task force, and moved the 2026 budget. Voted against ROC Eastman's book sales and the apartment beside the hospital, and argued 1:1 parking suits seniors' housing.

Took part in 21 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A steady, cost-conscious vote for council's health steps, with dissents on land use rather than on health itself.
