Health and wellbeing · Council member

John Fehr: health care and healthy living

What did John Fehr say and do about health care and healthy living in Steinbach (doctors and clinics, emergency services, mental health, seniors, recreation) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr praised the Bethesda Foundation in a 2016 opening for securing health-care funding for the region, and seconded the 2018 variance that let Rest Haven add 142 care-home beds, saying there had long been pressure for more.

On money he held to the city's grants policy: council was 'typically not interested in providing operating grants', he said when moving the golf club land purchase in 2017. He was reported as voting against dropping the multiplex in 2016.

“This is something that we have long had much pressure to add personal care beds in this community.”

Councillor John Fehr · Seconding the Rest Haven care-home variance · Apr 3, 2018 · 0:27:33

Term by term

2014–2018

Praised the Bethesda Foundation; chaired the meeting that approved signs for the new medical centre ('a good way to direct the traffic there'); moved the golf land purchase; seconded the Rest Haven variance; and, per SteinbachOnline, voted against dropping the multiplex.

Took part in 12 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

A short but consistent health record: support for health facilities through land-use decisions, and no operating grants.
