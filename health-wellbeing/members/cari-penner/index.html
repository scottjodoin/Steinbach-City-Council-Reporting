Cari Penner: health care and healthy living

What did Cari Penner say and do about health care and healthy living in Steinbach (doctors and clinics, emergency services, mental health, seniors, recreation) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner's health record is thin and partly uncertain, because in 2015–2017 the two Councillors Penner often cannot be told apart in the recordings. The RCMP credited both Penners with helping start the Headway youth program through the RCMP advisory committee.

As deputy mayor she chaired the November 2017 meeting that took the Headway, Katie Cares and Youth for Christ letters as information. She moved the 2018 grant application for Aquatic Centre upgrades and seconded the 2018 budget.
