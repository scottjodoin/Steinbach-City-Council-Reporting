Cari Penner: crime, policing and community safety

What did Cari Penner say and do about crime, policing and community safety in Steinbach (the RCMP contract and its cost, policing priorities, safety officers and private security) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner's record on policing is thin and partly uncertain. The RCMP staff sergeant named her (with Susan Penner) in 2016 as instrumental in bringing the Headway youth program together through the RCMP Advisory Committee. As Deputy Mayor she chaired the November 2017 meeting where an RCMP letter on Headway funding was taken as information.

She seconded the 2018 financial plan, whose 0.16-mill increase was tied by its mover to the higher RCMP cost share. Several questions to the RCMP in 2016–2017 came from 'Councillor Penner', but the recordings do not show which Penner spoke, so they are not attributed to her.

2016RCMP reports thefts halved, a meth problem and reduced traffic services (Apr 2016)Presenting occurrence statistics on Apr 5, 2016, the staff sergeant credited the city-funded two-officer General Investigation Section (GIS) unit for drug and theft results, said there was a meth issue driving minor thefts and mischief, and said RCMP traffic services in Steinbach had been much reduced by RCMP repositioning, so he had lobbied headquarters for more patrols. The mayor noted thefts and vehicle thefts had fallen by more than half from 2014 to 2015.Done Report received; council responded by reconfirming the same priorities (see 2016-policing-priorities). 2016Mental Health Act change frees RCMP officers from waiting at hospitalOn Jul 5, 2016 a Councillor Penner reported that a provincial amendment to the Mental Health Act, carried by Steinbach MLA and Health Minister Kelvin Goertzen, lets someone other than a police officer stay with a person waiting for a mental-health assessment. The idea came from the city's RCMP Advisory Group and the staff sergeant, who had found officers waiting hours at the hospital.Done Amendment passed by the Legislature (as reported to council); council recorded thanks.
