Bill Hiebert: crime, policing and community safety

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about crime, policing and community safety in Steinbach (the RCMP contract and its cost, policing priorities, safety officers and private security) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert made few recorded statements on policing. In June 2020, after the killing of George Floyd, he spoke against racism and praised Steinbach's police for fair treatment. He seconded routine motions, such as the 2020 transfer to a policing reserve, the second reading of the 2022 tax by-law that paid for the traffic officer, and the 2022 advisory group appointments.

Some questions to the RCMP (about the extra traffic constable in 2023 and patrol hours in 2025) may be his, but the recordings mix his voice with Jake Hiebert's, so they are not attributed to him. Every policing vote he took part in was unanimous.

“In my closing, I want to emphasize that we have a great police force here in Steinbach that believes in racial equality, and they do their best in delivering fair treatment to all.”

Councillor Bill Hiebert · Opening remarks after the killing of George Floyd · Jun 16, 2020 · 0:00:15
