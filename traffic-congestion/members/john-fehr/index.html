Traffic and congestion · Council member

John Fehr: traffic, congestion and road safety

What did John Fehr say and do about traffic congestion and road safety in Steinbach (Main Street and the highways, city streets, stop signs and signals, speed and enforcement) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr moved the 2016 contract for the Penfeld and Acres Drive intersection on PTH 12, saying council had made it a priority to make Steinbach safer, and seconded the full Old Tom Road paving because delay would only raise costs. He moved the longer no-parking zone on Reimer Avenue West, noting that narrow streets were becoming collector lanes, and floated a stop sign at Albert Street and Loewen Boulevard for residents.

In 2018 he moved the road-widening by-law that secured land at Loewen Boulevard and PTH 12 for future turn lanes, citing truck traffic on the tight corner, and asked that lane lines at McKenzie and Brandt be repainted regularly. In a 2017 discussion of school zones a speaker identified with medium confidence as Fehr cautioned against all-day 30 km/h zones that would slow traffic. His term ended in 2018.

“This council made a priority to make Steinbach a safer place to be, and this is another step in this process.”
