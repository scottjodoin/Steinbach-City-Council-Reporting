Traffic and congestion · Council member

Chris Goertzen: traffic, congestion and road safety

What did Chris Goertzen say and do about traffic congestion and road safety in Steinbach (Main Street and the highways, city streets, stop signs and signals, speed and enforcement) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

In short

Chris Goertzen chaired council as the three-year PTH 12 intersection program, cost-shared with the province, finished in 2016 with signals at Penfeld and Acres Drive. He prompted council's 2017 request to the province for signals at Industrial Road and PTH 52 and, as a quicker step, the request to the Highway Traffic Board to move the PTH 52 West speed zone, and welcomed the province's go-ahead for the signals in March 2018.

He repeatedly framed safety as driver behaviour and enforcement as well as roads: a no-parking rule is 'only going to be valuable' if enforced, and 'no matter how many traffic lights and crosswalks we put in' drivers must be careful. He cited cost for not widening Henry Street, and described securing land at Loewen Boulevard for future turn lanes as part of long-term traffic planning. His term ended in October 2018.

“Council, just a reminder that no matter how many traffic lights and crosswalks we put in, it also requires drivers to be Good and diligent, and we need to also see that happen.”

Mayor Chris Goertzen · After council asked the province for signals at Industrial Road and crosswalks on PTH 12 · Jan 17, 2017 · 0:21:27

Term by term

2014–2018

Chaired the final year of the PTH 12 program (Penfeld/Acres signals, 2016), calling Highway 12 'a safer street and a more efficient street'. Suggested the 2017 Highway Traffic Board request on PTH 52 West, saying the board's decisions are 'not always evidence-based', and pressed for the Industrial Road signals, approved in 2018. Kept asking the province for a PTH 12 crossing near the Christian school; councillors said in 2023 it still had not met the count warrant. Stressed enforcement on Main Street East parking, cited cost against widening Henry Street, and backed the 2018 land deal for future turn lanes at Loewen Boulevard.

Took part in 13 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

Council's PTH 12 partnership with the province delivered the most intersection work of any term in this record during his years; the corners it did not reach, Loewen Boulevard and the PTH 12 crossings, were left to the next council.
