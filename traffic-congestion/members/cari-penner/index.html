Traffic and congestion · Council member

Cari Penner: traffic, congestion and road safety

What did Cari Penner say and do about traffic congestion and road safety in Steinbach (Main Street and the highways, city streets, stop signs and signals, speed and enforcement) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner's one clearly attributed traffic initiative is the February 2018 motion to join the province's cost-share program to replace the pedestrian crossing bands on Main Street at Elm Avenue, William Avenue and the Elmdale School crossing. In August 2018 the province wrote that no funding was available, and nothing further was recorded. She also seconded first reading of the 2018 Loewen Boulevard widening by-law.

Several 2016–2017 requests were moved by 'Councillor Penner' without a first name: the PTH 52 West speed review, crosswalks on PTH 12, Park Road West and Henry Street. They may be hers or Susan Penner's, and are not attributed to either here. Her term ended in 2018.
