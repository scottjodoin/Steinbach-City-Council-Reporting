Traffic and congestion · Council member

Jac Siemens: traffic, congestion and road safety

What did Jac Siemens say and do about traffic congestion and road safety in Steinbach (Main Street and the highways, city streets, stop signs and signals, speed and enforcement) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

In short

Jac Siemens raised congestion more often than any other member: four lanes on PTH 12 for Summer in the City (2016), a count at Loewen and Hespeler after being stuck in traffic (2017 and again in 2023), badly timed PTH 12 signals (2023, fixed by the province within four months), and the slow Giesbrecht lights. He moved the 2018 four-way stop at Barkman and Fourth against staff advice and the 2024 three-way stop at Loewen and Hespeler, which lost 3–4. He said he had promoted a roundabout there first.

He was not a reflexive yes to residents. He said he was 'not a big fan' of stop signs, moved to refuse the Wyndham stop sign under council's guideline, opposed signals at PTH 52 and Lund Road, and argued from the counts that 'traffic comes to Steinbach', so a bypass made no sense. He voted against several rezonings partly over traffic, asked when traffic studies are required, and called the city's active transportation planning incomplete. He carried council's highway lobbying through the Eastman regional committee. He did not run in 2026.

“The traffic around Steinbach is horrendous. … when we paved Old Tom 10 years ago, whenever that was, there was a lot of unintended consequences, and that becomes a way to bypass Highway 12 and our main street.”

Councillor Jac Siemens · At the hearing on the Old Tom Road and Loewen Boulevard neighbourhood · Sept 6, 2022 · 1:18:26

Term by term

2014–2018

Asked that four PTH 12 lanes stay open for Summer in the City, asked for a count at Loewen and Hespeler after two traffic jams, and pressed for a traffic impact study at a Loewen Boulevard rezoning. Moved the only contested traffic amendment of the term, a stricter no-parking zone on Main Street East (defeated). In January 2018 moved the four-way stop at Barkman Avenue and Fourth Street against staff advice (unanimous). Raised the daily collisions on Brandt Street with the RCMP.

Took part in 15 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

2018–2022

Championed a path for Clearspring Greens from 2018 but voted against making residents pay most of it (lost 2–5 and 5–2, then 6–1). Moved the McKenzie–Giesbrecht crossing while saying he was 'not a big fan' of stop signs. Asked when traffic studies are required and wanted one for the next First Street project, but voted for the 2021 rezoning. Extended the engine-brake sign request to all four highways. Said in 2022 that traffic was 'horrendous' and that there was 'not a definitive plan' for Old Tom and Loewen; voted against 6 First Street and the McKenzie fourplex partly on traffic grounds.

Took part in 18 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Raised the PTH 12 signal timing in January 2023 and confirmed the fix that spring; suggested overnight flashing signals, which provincial policy forbids. Asked for a new count at Loewen and Hespeler, then moved the three-way stop (defeated 3–4). Moved to deny the Wyndham stop sign under council's guideline, voted alone against the SRSS fourplex over congestion (2024), asked for traffic calming at Harmony Lane, and sought a sidewalk condition on a McKenzie Avenue rezoning. Opposed lights at PTH 52 and Lund Road, dismissed a bypass, wanted parking removed from PTH 52, moved the community plan and Active Transportation Plan, and lobbied ministers on Highways 12, 52 and 59 through the regional committee.

Took part in 27 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

Consistently the member closest to drivers' and residents' complaints, and the most open to changing the road (stop signs, a roundabout, removing parking). He paired that with a belief in counts and guidelines, so he sometimes argued for a resident's request and sometimes against it.
