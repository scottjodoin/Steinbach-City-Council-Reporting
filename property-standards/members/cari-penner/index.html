Cari Penner: property standards and enforcement

What did Cari Penner say and do about property standards and their enforcement in Steinbach (yards, garbage, rental buildings, secondary suites) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

As Deputy Mayor, Cari Penner chaired the August 2018 hearing on rezoning a row of old rental houses on Maplewood Street, where neighbours objected to how the owner had kept them.

She said the project was 'renewing a neighborhood that needs some help', but voted with the majority to deny it. A Councillor Penner, probably Cari Penner, moved second reading of the 2017 administrative penalty by-law; the identification is not certain, and no remark from that meeting is attributed to her here.

“For myself, I feel that this is a good thing to do in the fact that it is renewing a neighborhood that needs some help.”

Councillor Cari Penner · Chairing the Maplewood Street rezoning hearing · Aug 21, 2018 · 1:02:54
