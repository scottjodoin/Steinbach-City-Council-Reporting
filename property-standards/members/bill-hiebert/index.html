Bill Hiebert: property standards and enforcement

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about property standards and their enforcement in Steinbach (yards, garbage, rental buildings, secondary suites) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert's part in this record is mostly routine: he moved the 2021 weed-inspector appointment, seconded the 2022 and 2026 ones, and seconded readings of penalty-schedule amendments. He voted for By-law 2157 in 2021.

In August 2024 he backed proactive enforcement of the suite-safety by-law and said he would go 'one step further', upgrading egress windows even in family homes with basement bedrooms, adding that safety matters most where people pay rent.

“But especially in rental units where people are paying rent, to have safety is so important.”

Councillor Bill Hiebert · Supporting proactive suite enforcement · Aug 20, 2024 · 0:24:09
