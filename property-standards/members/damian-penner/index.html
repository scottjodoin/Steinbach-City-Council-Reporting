Property standards · Council member

Damian Penner: property standards and enforcement

What did Damian Penner say and do about property standards and their enforcement in Steinbach (yards, garbage, rental buildings, secondary suites) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Damian Penner made no recorded comment in the August 2024 discussion of suite enforcement, but voted for By-law 2157 in 2021 and seconded or moved several penalty-schedule amendments.

In March 2024 he said infill applications usually come to areas 'neglected for years, sometimes decades', describing redevelopment as the answer to neglected housing. Moving By-law 2282 in 2026, he set out the city's approach: 'first educate and then enforce and then penalize', adding that inspectors were not out looking for people to penalize.

“the history of the city has been, and I hope will continue to be, to first educate and then enforce and then penalize. … This isn't to have our inspectors going out looking for individuals to penalize, but rather to have the opportunity to encourage them stronger to get their work done.”

Councillor Damian Penner · Moving the 2026 penalty-schedule amendment · Aug 4, 2026 · 1:25:40
