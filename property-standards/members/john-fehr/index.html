Property standards · Council member

John Fehr: property standards and enforcement

What did John Fehr say and do about property standards and their enforcement in Steinbach (yards, garbage, rental buildings, secondary suites) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr's only recorded part in this topic came at the August 2018 Maplewood Street hearing, where neighbours said an owner had let a row of rental houses run down.

He said the case 'always tears me up': the properties needed attention and someone wanted to redevelop them, yet met resistance. He seconded the motion to deny the rezoning, which carried 5–1. Council used no property-standards tool on the existing houses.

“This is something that always tears me up because we have a row of properties here that really need to have some attention, and here's somebody that comes in and wants to do something with it, and then it's met with some resistance”

Councillor John Fehr · At the Maplewood Street rezoning hearing · Aug 21, 2018 · 0:57:39
