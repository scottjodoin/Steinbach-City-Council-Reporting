Jake Hiebert: property standards and enforcement

What did Jake Hiebert say and do about property standards and their enforcement in Steinbach (yards, garbage, rental buildings, secondary suites) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Jake Hiebert moved the 2020 weed-inspector appointment, seconded third reading of By-law 2157 in 2021, and moved or seconded several penalty-schedule readings, all routine.

His clearest statement came in August 2024. He said he had been 'quite disgusted' at the number of non-compliant secondary suites he saw while campaigning in 2018, that the fire chief had issued warnings without results, and that he was '100% in favor' of proactive enforcement.

“Going back to when we campaigned in 2018, I'll be Quite frank, I was quite disgusted at the number of homes I saw where there was delinquent secondary suites.”

Councillor Jake Hiebert · On non-compliant secondary suites · Aug 20, 2024 · 0:19:40

It sets minimum life-safety standards for every dwelling unit: smoke alarms, carbon monoxide alarms and a safe exit, with secondary (basement) suites the main target. Staff said compliance was expected to be mostly voluntary, with inspections when owners or tenants ask; council members said the rules were basic and not onerous.Done Adopted unanimously.
