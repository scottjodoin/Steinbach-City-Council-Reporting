Cari Penner: taxes, budgets and spending

What did Cari Penner say and do about Steinbach's taxes, budgets, debt and spending between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner's remarks on money can be attributed with certainty mainly in 2018, after minutes begin. She seconded the 2018 budget, praising lower debt payments and 'debt management… well within the guidelines,' moved its tax by-law readings and reserve transfers, and moved two of the downtown land purchases as part of 'the long-term strategy.'

She is inferred to have supported the golf land deal in 2017, and voted against tabling the Arts Centre's $65,000 matching grant in 2018. Before 2018, many remarks by 'Councillor Penner' could be hers or Susan Penner's and are not attributed.

“one thing that I'm very happy to see is our debt payments are lower this year. Our debt management is very adequate and well within the guidelines, and our reserves are still still in a healthy way.”

Councillor Cari Penner · Seconding the 2018 budget · Mar 20, 2018 · 0:23:30

Term by term

2014–2018

Served on the 2016 performing arts centre task force and seconded the December 2016 reaffirmation of the city's $7.5 million share (4–3). In 2018 she seconded the budget, moved its levy by-law and reserve transfers, moved downtown land purchases, and seconded the Arts Centre match. Inferred to have voted for the golf land purchase (December 2017). Did not run in 2018.

Took part in 11 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

A supporter of Mayor Goertzen's majority on the contested money votes of 2017–2018.
