Taxes and spending · Council member

Bill Hiebert: taxes, budgets and spending

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about Steinbach's taxes, budgets, debt and spending between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert has voted with the majority on every budget, borrowing and fee decision in this record, and his recorded remarks on money are brief. He supported the event centre throughout, saying the arena was a main reason he ran for council and that the borrowing would be 'well worth it for our community.'

He moved the 2023 change order that raised the event centre's cost to $69.2 million, credited earlier councils' reserves for the 2024 budget, seconded the end of the economic development grant in 2025, and supported the $400,000 loan to the event centre operator. Several of his 2025 remarks could not be attributed with confidence and are not quoted.

“even though it's $7.5 million, a lot of money, I know that. But it's going to be well worth it for our community.”

Councillor Bill Hiebert · On borrowing $7.5 million for the event centre · Dec 7, 2021 · 0:11:39

Term by term

2018–2022

Voted against council laptops, voted to delay the 2019 purchasing by-law for more study, and voted with the majority on the Clearspring Greens levy. Seconded the event centre agreement and supported its borrowing.

Took part in 23 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Voted for 5% development fees (seconded second reading), moved the event centre change order, and credited the recreation reserve in the 2024 budget. Excused from the March 4, 2025 hearing but seconded the final 2025 plan. Seconded ending the economic development grant and supported repaying the operator loan over four years. Re-elected in 2026.

Took part in 24 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A quiet, consistent vote with the majority on money across two terms, most engaged on the event centre.
