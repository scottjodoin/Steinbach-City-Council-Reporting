A Main Street bypass · Council member

Bill Hiebert: a bypass and through traffic

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert's main contribution on this topic was about trucks. In July 2022 he passed on complaints from residents south of the city about engine-brake noise early and late, and moved the resulting request to the province for 'no engine brake' signs (R22-177). No reply or installation was reported; the city's own 2025 traffic by-law banned engine brakes.

At the March 2023 meeting that approved the Park Road West extension, a member identified with medium confidence as him, and named by SteinbachOnline, said it would be 'a great bypass' while Loewen Boulevard was rebuilt. He has not spoken to a highway bypass on the record, and was re-elected in 2026.

“I've had a couple of citizens south of Steinbach complaining about engine brake noise. Early mornings, late nights, and just wondering if there's any way we can address that.”
