A Main Street bypass · Council member

John Fehr: a bypass and through traffic

What did John Fehr say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr's remarks on this topic were about trucks on the city's designated routes. In December 2017 he asked whether McKenzie Avenue, 'a very, very heavy truck traffic road', would be rebuilt in concrete; it was. In August 2018 he commended the deal that secured corner land at Loewen Boulevard and PTH 12, because 'it is a trucking route there'.

He seconded the 2016 Old Tom Road paving, saying delay would only raise costs, and moved first reading of the 2018 development plan (routine), which treated the highways through Steinbach as a major corridor with no bypass policy. His term ended in October 2018.
