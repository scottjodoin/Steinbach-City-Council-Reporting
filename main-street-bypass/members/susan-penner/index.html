A Main Street bypass · Council member

Susan Penner: a bypass and through traffic

What did Susan Penner say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor in all three terms covered · Deputy Mayor from November 2026 (topped the 2026 poll)

In short

Susan Penner's part in this topic was practical. She moved the 2023 Park Road West extension, describing how it would connect Park Road West to Industrial Road in pavement, and in 2024 asked whether a proposed truck yard's trucks would stay on PTH 12 and 52 rather than residential streets.

She moved the December 2024 objection to the RM of Hanover's rezoning on PTH 12 North, which the city said ignored the 2018 highway access plan, and the third reading of the 2025 development plan (routine). She has not spoken to a bypass on the record. She topped the 2026 poll and is Deputy Mayor of the new council.

“My understanding is just based on the truck routes in the city that the traffic would be on the number 12 and on the number 52, that Hespeler would— Hespeler and Lake Lomond Boulevard would not be used.”

Councillor Susan Penner · Asking where a proposed truck yard's trucks would drive · Jan 16, 2024 · 1:12:29

No recorded part in the bypass or truck items of this term beyond routine votes. A 2016 question about detour traffic on Park Road West came from a Councillor Penner who may have been her or Cari Penner.

2022–2026

Moved the Park Road West extension to Industrial Road (2023), asked about truck routes for the Tower Avenue truck yard (2024), moved the objection to Hanover's PTH 12 North rezoning (2024) and the third reading of the 2025 development plan.

Took part in 3 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A steady, procedural record on the topic. As Deputy Mayor of the new council she is one of the members who will decide whether the through-traffic question is ever measured.
