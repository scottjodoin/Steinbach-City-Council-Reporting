A Main Street bypass · Council member

Earl Funk: a bypass and through traffic

What did Earl Funk say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Mayor since November 2018 (re-elected by acclamation in 2026)

In short

As mayor since November 2018, Earl Funk has chaired every decision on through traffic, and none was about a bypass. His approach was to make the highways through town carry more: he called the Loewen Boulevard and PTH 12 rebuild the city's priority with the province and said it would move 'a lot more traffic' more efficiently, and in 2026 he said the highway is there to move people in and out but they are 'stopping at every light'. He welcomed Park Road West as 'another way to go north' (2023).

On the city's own edge roads he took the line that roads follow development. He told Clearspring Greens residents in 2019 that the budget had no room, told Old Tom and Loewen residents in 2022 that 'we let the development happen and then we can work on the road system', and in 2020 said council needed a plan for how its edge roads would handle growth; no such plan came to a public meeting. He noted in 2026 that commuters from the west congest PTH 52 each morning, but did not respond when Councillor Siemens dismissed a bypass.

“We got people, we want to move people, and then the highway's there to move people, to move people in and move people out. And right now they're, they're stopping at every light.”

Mayor Earl Funk · On the 2025 traffic count and the PTH 12 signals · Feb 17, 2026 · 0:21:15

Term by term

2014–2018

As a councillor he seconded acceptance of the 2016 highway count (routine) and took no other recorded part in the bypass or truck items of this term; the Old Tom Road paving carried without opposition.

Took part in 3 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

2018–2022

Told Clearspring Greens residents the 2019 budget 'did not allow us to do anything' about the edge road they called a perimeter road, promised in 2020 a plan for edge roads such as Bush Farm Road (none came to a public meeting), backed the Loewen and PTH 12 rebuild as moving more traffic more efficiently, said the yearly count helps prioritize intersections, and told the Old Tom–Loewen hearing that roads follow development.

Took part in 4 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Called the Park Road West extension 'another way to go north' (2023), pressed for PTH 12 signal timing, said in February 2026 that commuters from the west congest PTH 52 and that people are 'stopping at every light', and chaired the meeting where the bypass was dismissed without discussion.

Took part in 2 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

His view did not change: the highways through Steinbach are its main arteries, to be upgraded with the province, and city roads follow development. The plan for the edge roads he called for in 2020 is still outstanding as he begins a third term as mayor.
