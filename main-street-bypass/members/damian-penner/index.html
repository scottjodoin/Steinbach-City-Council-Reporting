A Main Street bypass · Council member

Damian Penner: a bypass and through traffic

What did Damian Penner say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Damian Penner backed fixing the highway rather than going around it. At the 2021 hearing on the Loewen Boulevard and PTH 12 rebuild he said it would 'alleviate some of the arterial roads and put it on our main road', and he seconded several years of the traffic count, noting in 2022 that growing Mitchell, to the west, was sending 'a lot more traffic going through'.

He seconded the Park Road West extension in 2023, crediting the city's healthy reserves, moved the 2022 Old Tom–Loewen subdivision approval (routine) and seconded the denial of the Tower Avenue truck yard. He has not spoken to a bypass on the record, and was re-elected in 2026.

“It will probably change traffic patterns to a certain extent and alleviate some of the arterial roads and put it on our main road.”

Councillor Damian Penner · On rebuilding Loewen Boulevard and PTH 12 · Dec 7, 2021 · 0:29:15
