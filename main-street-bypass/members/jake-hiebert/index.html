Jake Hiebert: a bypass and through traffic

What did Jake Hiebert say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Jake Hiebert's part in this topic was routine: he moved acceptance of the 2023 traffic count (R24-054), seconded others, including the February 2026 count at which the bypass was dismissed, and moved second reading of the 2025 development plan. He seconded council's 2019 support for twinning PTH 59. He has not spoken to a bypass, truck routes or through traffic on the record.

At the November 3, 2026 inaugural meeting the mayor named him to the Eastman regional committee that lobbies the province on highways, the role Jac Siemens held. That makes him, for the new term, council's link to the regional highway conversation.
