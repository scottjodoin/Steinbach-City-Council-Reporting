A Main Street bypass · Council member

Chris Goertzen: a bypass and through traffic

What did Chris Goertzen say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

In short

Chris Goertzen chaired council in 2016 when Old Tom Road was paved as a boundary road and PTH 12 construction pushed detour traffic onto gravel Park Road West. When staff said road improvements are developer-driven, he asked whether there was also 'a component that we are responsible for', the one recorded remark by him in this record.

His council rebuilt McKenzie Avenue in concrete for truck traffic and adopted the 2018 development plan, which treated PTH 12 and 52 through Steinbach as a major highway corridor. When a provincial official called the perimeter highway in the 2018 access plan 'just a hypothetical concept', no member of his council raised it at a meeting. His term ended in October 2018.

In his last years as mayor the city's edge roads began to work as an informal bypass; the question of who plans and pays for them passed to the next council.
