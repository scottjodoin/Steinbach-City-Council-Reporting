A Main Street bypass · Council members

Council members on a bypass and through traffic

What did each council member say and do about a bypass, truck routes and through traffic on Main Street and the highways between December 2015 and November 2026?

In short

Ten people served on council in these years; two more joined in November 2026. No vote on this topic divided council, and only one member ever spoke to a highway bypass, so the differences are in who described the problem and who moved the decisions.

  • Jac Siemens was the only voice on a bypass, and he was against it: in February 2026 he said the counts show “traffic comes to Steinbach.” He was also the member who most often described city streets as bypasses and raised trucks on Main Street. He did not run in 2026.
  • Michael Zwaagstra championed the yearly count as evidence for the province on intersections, but never proposed using it, or a new survey, to test the through-traffic question. He did not run in 2026.
  • Mayor Earl Funk wanted the highways to move more traffic, backed the Loewen rebuild and a second route north, and said roads follow development. His 2020 promise of a plan for the edge roads is unmet.
  • Damian Penner, Susan Penner, Bill Hiebert and Jake Hiebert mostly moved or seconded decisions; Damian Penner noted traffic “going through” from Mitchell, and Bill Hiebert raised trucks’ engine brakes.
  • Before 2018, Mayor Chris Goertzen asked whether the city should help pay for Park Road West, and John Fehr pressed for truck routes built to carry trucks. Cari Penner has no remark on the topic that could be attributed to her with certainty.

Choose a member

Earl Funk

Councillor 2010–2018 · Mayor since November 2018 (re-elected by acclamation in 2026)

Treated PTH 12 and Main Street as the routes 'to move people in and move people out': pushed to rebuild Loewen Boulevard and paved a second route north, but promised a plan for the edge roads that never appeared and never spoke to a bypass.

Chris Goertzen

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

Chaired the paving of Old Tom Road and asked whether the city should share the cost of Park Road West; no recorded remark on a bypass, including when the province called one hypothetical in 2018.

Michael Zwaagstra

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

Moved most of the yearly traffic counts and framed them as evidence for the province on intersections; backed regional highway twinning and corridor discipline on PTH 12 North, but never addressed a bypass.

Jac Siemens

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

The only member to speak to a highway bypass, dismissing it in 2026 because 'traffic comes to Steinbach'; also the member who most often described city streets as bypasses and raised trucks on Main Street.

Susan Penner

Councillor in all three terms covered · Deputy Mayor from November 2026 (topped the 2026 poll)

Moved the Park Road West extension and the objection to Hanover's PTH 12 North rezoning, and checked that a truck yard's trucks would stay on the highways; no recorded view on a bypass.

Damian Penner

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Said the Loewen rebuild would take traffic off arterial roads and 'put it on our main road', and noted more traffic 'going through' from growing Mitchell; no recorded view on a bypass.

Bill Hiebert

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Raised residents' complaints about trucks' engine brakes, which led to council's request for signs at all four highway entrances; probably the member who called Park Road West 'a great bypass'.

Jake Hiebert

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Moved or seconded several traffic-count acceptances and the 2025 development plan's second reading, all routine; no recorded view on a bypass or truck routes. Named to the regional highway committee in November 2026.

John Fehr

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

Asked that McKenzie Avenue, 'a very, very heavy truck traffic road', be rebuilt in concrete and welcomed securing the Loewen corner because 'it is a trucking route'; seconded paving Old Tom Road.

Ron Falk and Greg Carlos

Councillors since November 2026

Elected in October 2026; no record on this topic yet. Ron Falk was a Hanover School Division trustee for 34 years, 14 as chair; Greg Carlos is a past president of the South Eastman Filipino Association, and council is his first elected office.
