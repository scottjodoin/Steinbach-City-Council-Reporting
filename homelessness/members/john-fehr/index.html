Homelessness · Council member

John Fehr: homelessness and shelter

What did John Fehr say and do about homelessness in Steinbach (shelters, transitional housing, outreach, encampments) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

In a July 2016 opening John Fehr credited churches and charities, naming Today House, Soups On, Helping Hands and Agape House among others, for the social, practical and spiritual help they give 'the less fortunate.' Relying on charities was the period's prevailing approach.

In March 2018 he opened a meeting with an account of a man he found sleeping behind his business, who told him he had lived in the yard for five days the previous October without anyone noticing. He called him 'one of many homeless people in this community' and said he felt helpless to do more than offer coffee and toast. He also gave his own view of why this man had become homeless (not quoted here because it concerns a private individual; the full opening is in the meeting video, Mar 20, 2018, from 0:00:36). No discussion or motion followed. He left council in 2018.

Term by term

2014–2018

He credited churches and charities for helping people in need (2016) and, in a 2018 opening, described a man living behind his business as 'one of many homeless people in this community.' He proposed no city action, and none followed.

Took part in 3 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

He named the problem in public before anyone else on council, but proposed no city action.
