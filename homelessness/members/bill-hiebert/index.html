Bill Hiebert: homelessness and shelter

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about homelessness in Steinbach (shelters, transitional housing, outreach, encampments) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert joined council in November 2018. He moved the 2018 grants list (routine) and voted for every item in this record, all of them unanimous: The Bridge's grant and parking variance, the safety plan, the shelter and transitional-housing zoning changes and the Bridge on Third endorsement.

He voted with the rest of council to take no action on SCO's Emergency Treatment Fund request (2024); the minutes name the seconder in a way that may mean him or Councillor Siemens. His one recorded remark near the topic was sympathy for Adult & Teen Challenge, an addiction-recovery program, when he voted to deny it a site in the industrial park (2025). Re-elected in 2026.

“Mr. Mayor, I totally feel for Team Challenge. I would love to see them have a facility in Steinbach, and I'm hoping that you can find a spot where you can build something that will work for you.”

Councillor Bill Hiebert · Voting to deny Adult & Teen Challenge a site in the industrial park · Feb 18, 2025 · 0:38:30
