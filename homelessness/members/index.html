Homelessness · Council members

Council members on homelessness

How did each council member speak and vote on homelessness, shelters and transitional housing in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026?

In short

Ten people served on council in these years; two more joined in November 2026. No vote on homelessness ever divided council, so the differences between members show up in what they said and which motions they moved.

  • Before 2021, one voice. Councillor John Fehr was the only member to say on the record that Steinbach had “many homeless people” (2018). No one proposed city action.
  • The Bridge had broad support. Michael Zwaagstra moved the 2021 grant choice, Jac Siemens moved the 2022 variance, grant and by-law, and Susan Penner seconded; all seven members voted for it.
  • Different emphases since 2024. Jake Hiebert spoke most about what people living outside said they need, and doubted shelters met it. Zwaagstra was firmest against encampments and for public hearings. Susan Penner moved each zoning step that requires a hearing. Siemens called transitional housing “critical.” Mayor Earl Funk backed housing projects in principle while saying a homelessness strategy is not municipal jurisdiction.

Choose a member

Earl Funk

Councillor 2010–2018 · Mayor since November 2018 (re-elected by acclamation in 2026)

Backed The Bridge and, in principle, transitional housing; holds that a homelessness strategy 'doesn't fall under municipal jurisdiction' and wants every shelter or transitional-housing site to face a public hearing.

Chris Goertzen

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

Invited Eden Foundation in 2017 to explain the shortage of housing for single adults and asked whether something should be built; no city action followed.

Michael Zwaagstra

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

Led the 2021 choice of Steinbach Community Outreach for the provincial grant, then became council's firmest voice against encampments and for public hearings on shelters and transitional housing.

Jac Siemens

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

Moved the motions that built The Bridge and called transitional housing 'critical,' while asking how many tax dollars should go to social issues.

Susan Penner

Councillor in all three terms covered · Deputy Mayor from November 2026 (topped the 2026 poll)

Backed The Bridge and the Bridge on Third, and moved each step that made shelters and transitional housing need a public hearing.

Damian Penner

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Seconded the shelter zoning change and argued that parts of a zone may not suit a shelter; otherwise voted without comment.

Bill Hiebert

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Voted with the unanimous majority on every homelessness item; no substantive recorded comment on homelessness itself.

Jake Hiebert

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Spoke most directly about people living outside in Steinbach, saying they need long-term housing, jobs and treatment rather than food drop-offs or a cold-night shelter.

John Fehr

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

The only member before 2021 to say on the record that Steinbach has 'many homeless people,' after finding a man sleeping behind his business.

Cari Penner

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

No attributable statement on homelessness; seconded the 2017 grants list, which included $300 for Today House and $200 for Eden Foundation.

Ron Falk and Greg Carlos

Councillors since November 2026

Elected in October 2026; no record on this topic yet. Ron Falk was a Hanover School Division trustee for 34 years, 14 as chair; Greg Carlos is a past president of the South Eastman Filipino Association, and council is his first elected office.

Every recorded split vote on homelessness
