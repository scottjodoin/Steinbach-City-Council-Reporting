Homelessness · Council member

Susan Penner: homelessness and shelter

What did Susan Penner say and do about homelessness in Steinbach (shelters, transitional housing, outreach, encampments) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor in all three terms covered · Deputy Mayor from November 2026 (topped the 2026 poll)

In short

No remarks on this topic can be attributed to Susan Penner before 2018, when two Councillors Penner served. In 2021 she seconded the choice of Steinbach Community Outreach for the provincial grant, saying it was 'not just housing' but support as well, and in 2022 she seconded The Bridge's parking variance, noting that the parking by-law ignores how income affects car ownership.

From December 2024 she was the lead mover on zoning: the direction to make emergency shelters a conditional use, first reading of the zoning by-law, and first reading of transitional housing as a conditional use. Her reason was always the same: people living nearby should have a formal way to voice support or concern. At the 2025 hearing she asked staff whether the shelter label would affect funding. She moved the 2026 endorsement of the Bridge on Third, saying The Bridge is 'working,' and met the provincial housing minister with the mayor in June 2026. She is Deputy Mayor from November 2026.

“So while I agree there should be transitional housing, I think it should still be a conditional use because it's really important that we get feedback from the people who actually live in the area where the project will potentially go.”

Councillor Susan Penner · Moving first reading of the transitional-housing by-law · Apr 21, 2026 · 0:47:38

Term by term

2014–2018

No attributable statement on homelessness. Two Councillors Penner asked about the Eden suites in 2016 and could not be told apart in the recording.

Took part in 1 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

2018–2022

She seconded the 2018 grants list (routine), the 2021 choice of SCO for the provincial grant, The Bridge's 2022 parking variance and the first two readings of By-law 2210.

Took part in 3 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

She moved the December 2024 direction to make emergency shelters a conditional use, saying it followed The INN's announcement of a shelter on Main Street, and first reading of the zoning by-law in April 2025, when she also asked staff about the funding effect of the shelter label. She moved first reading of transitional housing as a conditional use (April 2026) and the Bridge on Third endorsement (May 2026), and seconded both private-security motions (2025). She voted with the rest of council to take no action on SCO's Emergency Treatment Fund request (2024).

Took part in 8 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A steady supporter of supportive housing run by agencies she trusts, and the member who most shaped the rule that any shelter or transitional housing must go through a public hearing.
