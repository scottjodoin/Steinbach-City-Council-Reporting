Advocacy report · Council member

Chris Goertzen: what they said and did when people asked council for something

What did Chris Goertzen say and do when residents and groups brought requests to Steinbach City Council between December 2015 and October 2026, and how did that change over time?

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

In short

As chair, Chris Goertzen usually settled a letter’s fate in a sentence: “take that as information,” or a staff report. He defended a “simplified and focused” grants policy, which meant groups asking for operating money, like the Pat Porter Active Living Centre, were turned down. He said citizens’ requests “are taken seriously” and that sometimes “that incites change” (2017), and small traffic fixes with staff support did pass quickly under him.

He supported buying the golf club’s land as “a win-win” (2017), proposed tabling the Arts Council’s request before supporting it (2018), voted against a Sunday-shopping plebiscite because he is “not a fan of governing by referendum,” and declared a conflict of interest and left the room for a 2016 drainage exception.

“for me, I'm not a fan of governing by referendum, and so I'll vote against it.”

On the Sunday-shopping plebiscite · Aug 7, 2018 · 0:40:08

The trajectory

His term ended in October 2018, when Earl Funk became mayor.
