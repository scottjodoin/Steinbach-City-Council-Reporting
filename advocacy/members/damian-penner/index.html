Advocacy report · Council member

Damian Penner: what they said and did when people asked council for something

What did Damian Penner say and do when residents and groups brought requests to Steinbach City Council between December 2015 and October 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Damian Penner most often moved requests that came through organizations he represents for council, like the Jake Epp Library, and he moved the economic development office (2021) and the Skimmers’ grant consents (2022, 2023). After the 2025 floods he said “it is up to us to now advocate for them.”

He also votes with the majority on cost and evidence: he moved the original Clearspring Greens levy (2020) before backing the lighting compromise, and voted against the Loewen–Hespeler stop (2024).

“Their current computer system is grossly outdated and in desperate need of renewal.”

Moving the library’s technology grant · Mar 19, 2019 · 0:56:36

Term by term

2018–2022

He moved the library’s one-time technology grant, describing its computers as “grossly outdated” (2019). On Clearspring Greens he moved the levy, then seconded the lighting compromise (2020). He answered residents’ questions about reopening the library during COVID (2020) and moved the economic development office (2021).

Took part in 9 of the requests in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

He moved the Skimmers’ grant consents and seconded the Pat Porter and Harv’s Air requests, and proposed looking into the RCMP Musical Ride’s costs (2022). He voted against the Loewen–Hespeler stop (2024), seconded the second disaster-aid resolution (2026) and moved first reading of the event centre loan, stressing it was “a loan … and not a grant” (2026).

Took part in 12 of the requests in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

Steady over two terms; re-elected in 2026.
