Bill Hiebert: what they said and did when people asked council for something

What did Bill Hiebert say and do when residents and groups brought requests to Steinbach City Council between December 2015 and October 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert is most visible when he relays a resident’s problem directly: engine-brake noise on the highways (2022, approved the same night) and hedges blocking sidewalks (2024). He seconded the event centre partnership (2019), backed its 2026 loan and voted for the Loewen–Hespeler stop (2024).

Fewer of his remarks could be attributed with certainty than for other members, partly because the transcription often mixes up the two Councillors Hiebert, so this record of him is thinner.

Term by term

2018–2022

He seconded the event centre agreement (2019) and voted with the majority on the Clearspring Greens and enVision levies (2020).

Took part in 10 of the requests in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

He raised residents’ calls about engine brakes (2022) and hedges over sidewalks (2024), voted for the Loewen–Hespeler stop (2024), seconded the golf club’s 2022 tax receipts and moved its 2026 ones, and said of the event centre loan that “year number 2 is already looking a lot better” (2026).

Took part in 11 of the requests in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

Steady over two terms; re-elected in 2026.
