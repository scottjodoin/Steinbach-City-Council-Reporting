Jake Hiebert: what they said and did when people asked council for something

What did Jake Hiebert say and do when residents and groups brought requests to Steinbach City Council between December 2015 and October 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Jake Hiebert rarely moves requests himself; he seconds many of them, including the Skimmers’ consents, the economic development office, the Chicken Chef patio and the Southwood school zone. His clearest stands came in split votes: with Jac Siemens he was one of only two votes for a cheaper Clearspring Greens pathway (2020), and he seconded and voted for the Loewen–Hespeler stop (2024). He voted for charging the enVision charity its levy (2020).

“I appreciate that this council has reconsidered in going forward with this compromise, what I will call a good compromise.”

On the Clearspring Greens lighting deal · Jan 21, 2020 · 0:16:39

Term by term

2018–2022

He voted for the cheaper Clearspring Greens amendment and against the levy, then called the lighting deal “a good compromise” (2020). He seconded the economic development office and the Chicken Chef patio (2021).

Took part in 14 of the requests in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

He seconded the Wyndham alternative (2023), the Loewen–Hespeler stop he voted for (2024) and the Southwood school zone (2025), seconded the refusal of the cannabis plebiscite (2025), and explained the legal rules for municipal loans when the event centre loan passed (2026).

Took part in 5 of the requests in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

Steady over two terms; re-elected in 2026.
