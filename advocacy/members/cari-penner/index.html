Cari Penner: what they said and did when people asked council for something

What did Cari Penner say and do when residents and groups brought requests to Steinbach City Council between December 2015 and October 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2014–2018 · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner seconded the 2016 drainage exception and the Arts Council’s request, which she spoke for strongly (2018), and with Jac Siemens cast the only votes for a Sunday-shopping plebiscite (2018). As acting chair in late 2017 she handled several letters, including a resident’s complaint about parking at a community mailbox.

Many remarks by “Councillor Penner” in 2015–2017 recordings could not be assigned to her or to Susan Penner, so her record here is incomplete.

“I, for one, am not afraid to put a referendum forward and ask the people what they would prefer, whether it's not or whether it's yes.”

On the Sunday-shopping plebiscite · Aug 7, 2018 · 0:37:14

The trajectory

Her term ended in October 2018.
