Advocacy report · Council members

Council members and requests from the public

How did each council member respond when residents and groups asked council for something between December 2015 and October 2026?

In short

Ten people served on council in these years; two more joined in November 2026. Most requests were decided unanimously, so the differences between members show up in a few places: who carried a request, what they asked, and the handful of split votes.

  • The mayors handle the most requests because they chair: they decide in practice whether a letter is discussed, referred or filed.
  • Michael Zwaagstra asked the cost-and-evidence questions and named the tests council applied; Susan Penner most often carried a request through and designed compromises.
  • In split votes, Jac Siemens was most often on the side of the person asking. He and Michael Zwaagstra, usually on opposite sides of those votes, both left council in 2026.

Choose a member

Earl Funk

Councillor 2010–2018 · Mayor since November 2018 (re-elected by acclamation in 2026)

As a councillor he pushed council to act for residents; as mayor he decides how most requests are handled, and he has driven the biggest yeses.

Chris Goertzen

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

Ran a tight process: most letters were filed or sent to staff, and grants followed a narrow policy.

Michael Zwaagstra

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

Council’s most active questioner: tough on cost and evidence, quick to approve cheap fixes, and the voice of “what does the report say?”

Jac Siemens

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

When council split, the member most often on the side of the person asking, and a persistent carrier of residents’ complaints, though careful with money and precedent.

Susan Penner

Councillor in all three terms covered · Deputy Mayor from November 2026 (topped the 2026 poll)

Often the councillor who takes a group’s request and carries it through, and who designs the compromise that settles it.

Damian Penner

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Brings requests from the boards he sits on, and talks about council’s duty to speak up for residents.

Bill Hiebert

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Brings individual residents’ complaints straight to council; fewer recorded remarks than most members.

Jake Hiebert

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

Usually the seconder; in the 2020 levy fight, one of only two votes for a cheaper option.

John Fehr

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

Took residents’ and business owners’ asks on himself, but backed the golf-club deal made in private.

Cari Penner

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

Backed the Arts Council and a Sunday-shopping plebiscite; many of her remarks can’t be separated from Susan Penner’s.

Ron Falk and Greg Carlos

Councillors since November 2026

Elected in October 2026; no record on requests yet. Ron Falk was a Hanover School Division trustee for 34 years, 14 as chair. Greg Carlos is a past president of the South Eastman Filipino Association; council is his first elected office.
