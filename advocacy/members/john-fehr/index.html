Advocacy report · Council member

John Fehr: what they said and did when people asked council for something

What did John Fehr say and do when residents and groups brought requests to Steinbach City Council between December 2015 and October 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2014–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr often took up requests personally: action on the Home Street North swale (2015), clearer paving rules for businesses on gravel roads and accessible parking on Main Street (2017), and a “more permanent step” for neighbours bothered by the Cottonwood Bend walkway (2018).

He moved the golf-club land purchase and defended handling it in closed meetings (2017), called the Arts Council’s first request “reactionary” before supporting it, and wanted “a lot more research” before a taxi by-law (2018).

“I would sure like to see us entertain doing something there to rectify that.”

On the Home Street North swale · Dec 15, 2015 · 0:30:27

The trajectory

His term ended in October 2018.
