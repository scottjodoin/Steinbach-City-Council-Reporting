Sewer backups and flooding · Council member

Bill Hiebert: sewer backups, flooding and drainage

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about basement sewer backups, flooding and drainage in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert seconded many sewer and drainage decisions, from the Deerfield detention pond (2019) and the 2022 capital program to the November 2024 request for disaster aid, the climate-plan grant application, the Clear Spring Greens reroute contract and third reading of the Land Drainage By-law. He made few statements on the topic. In 2023 he was the only vote against denying a side-yard variance that staff said could limit drainage, without giving a reason on the record.

In June 2026 he said 'drainage is a big issue in our community' after the last couple of years of major rains. At the Bridgepark hearing a speaker identified with medium confidence as him said his own house flooded both years and that the city is doing a lot to improve drainage; he voted for the building.

“drainage is a big issue in our community. As we've seen the last couple of years, when there's major rains, we need to have drainage”

Councillor Bill Hiebert · On keeping a new drain in city hands, June 2026 · Jun 2, 2026 · 1:18:46

Term by term

2018–2022

Seconded the Deerfield pond, the Kroeker Avenue tender and the 2022 capital works; voted to deny the Alexander Way variance on drainage advice. No substantive drainage statements identified with certainty.

Took part in 8 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Seconded the 2024 disaster-aid request and climate grant, the reroute contract and the drainage by-law. Lone vote against a 2023 variance denial on drainage grounds. Said in 2026 that drainage is a big issue after recent rains, and voted for Bridgepark.

Took part in 16 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A steady supporting vote for capital and drainage work, with little on the record about his own views until the 2026 hearings.
