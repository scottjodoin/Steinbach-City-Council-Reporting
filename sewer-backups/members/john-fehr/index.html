Sewer backups and flooding · Council member

John Fehr: sewer backups, flooding and drainage

What did John Fehr say and do about basement sewer backups, flooding and drainage in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr raised the Home Street North swale in December 2015, asking council to consider a culvert and some asphalt. As council's representative on the Seine-Rat River Conservation District he reported in 2016 that talks on holding water upstream of the Fly-In Golf Course had stalled, and pointed out that the creek there and the drain at Lowen Boulevard and Old Tom Road were provincial drains.

He insisted on drainage rules for individual lots, opposing a homeowner's request to waive a drainage plan ('stick to our guns'). In May 2018 he voted against a First Street infill variance, saying bigger buildings affect water flow and that people who had water in their basements in past floods find it hard to accept; he said his own children had been affected.

“it's very hard to explain it to somebody that has had a considerable amount of water in their basement when we've had flooding events. where the properties were not able to carry the water. And I know my kids were victims of that”

Councillor John Fehr · Voting against the First Street variance, May 2018 · May 1, 2018 · 0:50:18

Term by term

2014–2018

Asked for a Home Street North culvert (2015); chaired the 2016 swale-exception debate and supported it as a one-time decision; reported stalled upstream-retention talks and asked whether the province should share costs for its drains; seconded the denial of the Fernwood subdivision; opposed waiving a drainage plan; voted against the 2018 First Street variance over runoff; seconded the Maplewood denial.

Took part in 9 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

Consistently cautious about water: the member most likely to tie individual decisions to flooding in his years on council.
