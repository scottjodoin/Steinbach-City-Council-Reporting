Sewer backups and flooding · Council member

Cari Penner: sewer backups, flooding and drainage

What did Cari Penner say and do about basement sewer backups, flooding and drainage in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner seconded the 2016 one-time exception that let the Steinbach North commercial subdivision use roadside swales instead of curb, gutter and storm sewer, saying the swales in the city's industrial areas 'are working, I think, quite well.' In 2015–2017 the two Councillors Penner often cannot be told apart in the recordings, so some of her remarks are not attributed here.

As Deputy Mayor she chaired the August 2018 Maplewood hearing, telling residents that drainage 'will be regulated by the City of Steinbach as per our regulations', and voted with the majority to deny the rezoning. She voted against the 2018 First Street infill variance on neighbourhood-fit grounds.

“And really the major difference in the specs here is the swale. And looking at the industrial developments in our city, the swales are fine. The swales are working, I think, quite well.”

Councillor Cari Penner · Seconding the swale exception, May 2016 · May 3, 2016 · 0:21:25

2014–2018

The trajectory

Few recorded positions on this topic; supported lighter drainage standards for commercial land and the city's drainage rules for housing.
