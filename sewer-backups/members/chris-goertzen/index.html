Sewer backups and flooding · Council member

Chris Goertzen: sewer backups, flooding and drainage

What did Chris Goertzen say and do about basement sewer backups, flooding and drainage in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

In short

Mayor Chris Goertzen described the city's street-by-street replacement of old sewer and water mains as 'a long-term strategy' to be 'ahead of problems rather than reacting to problems.' When neighbours worried about sewer capacity or runoff from new buildings, he pointed to the engineering review, the developer's duty to pay for upgrades, and the rule that new subdivisions may send out no more water than before ('net zero outflow').

On residents' drainage requests he built consensus for staff reports rather than immediate work. The Home Street North swale went from a resident's email in December 2015 to a costed report in December 2017; he summed up the fix as 'over $1 million' and asked that petitioners be told the cost and the extra monitoring. In 2018 he named lagoon expansion as the city's 'next major hurdle.' No storm or basement-backup event was discussed at council during his years in this record.

“this is part of a long-term strategy that we've had and that we've been implementing over the last quite a number of years, and that is to renew our underground infrastructure and making sure that we are ahead of problems rather than reacting to problems.”

Mayor Chris Goertzen · On replacing old sewer and water mains · Dec 15, 2015 · 0:09:53

Term by term

2014–2018

Championed planned renewal of underground pipes; sent the Home Street North swale request to a staff report and relayed its cost reason ('We wouldn't design it that way now'); told the Fly-In Golf Course that new subdivisions must keep net-zero outflow and that much of its water came from the neighbouring municipality; voted against denying the Fernwood subdivision because approval would give the area a drainage plan; flagged lagoon expansion in 2018.

Took part in 9 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

Consistent: renewal on a schedule, drainage through development rules, and reports before spending on residents' requests.
