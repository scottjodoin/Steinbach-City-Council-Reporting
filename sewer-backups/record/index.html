Every decision and debate on sewer backups and flooding

What has Steinbach City Council decided, debated and promised about sewer backups, flooding and drainage since December 2015, and how did each item end?

In short

Each row is one decision, debate, promise or request on the topic, from the minutes and the meeting videos. Filter by term, year, theme, council member or result. “No public result” means we found no public decision or follow-up; some work happens in closed study sessions and committees.

2015–17Home Street North drainage swale: residents ask for a culvert; council studies it, then declines a fix costed at about $500,000Water from an open drain is designed to flow over Home Street North near Lowen Boulevard, leaving water and ice on the road. After an email from resident K.B. (Dec 2015), council asked administration to monitor and report; in Feb 2017 K.B. presented a petition asking for a culvert under the road, and council voted for a report with options and costs. The Dec 2017 report found a piped crossing would need the road raised about a metre, possibly property purchases, and cost roughly $500,000, so no fix was made; the city instead stepped up monitoring and maintenance.Declined No reconstruction. Administration's Dec 5, 2017 report judged a culvert/bridge option unreasonable on cost and property impact; the mayor asked that the cost figures and the increased monitoring be relayed to the petitioners. The report asked for by the Feb 2017 motion was targeted for the summer 2017 study session but came to council in December. K.B. raised the swale again at the Aug 21, 2018 Maplewood hearing.vote: carried (count not recorded); Dec 2015 report request was by consensus
