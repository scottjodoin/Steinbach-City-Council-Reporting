Quality of life · Council member

Jake Hiebert: quality of life

What did Jake Hiebert say and do about quality-of-life issues in Steinbach (noise, newcomers and inclusion, flags and proclamations, green space, arts and culture) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

In his first term Jake Hiebert sided with Jac Siemens on the Clearspring Greens pathway, seconding a cheaper limestone path and voting against the 2020 levy that charged residents most of the cost. He voted to enforce the street-edge tree rule both times and seconded support for the Arts Centre's provincial grant application.

In 2022–2026 he was a long-time advocate of a dedicated traffic officer, moved council's 2024 support in principle for a regional library, and at the mayor's request attended and reported on Steinbach's first Sikh celebration, saying he learned there were over 500 Sikh families in the city.

“I learned that there are over 500 Sikh families in Steinbach, and many were in attendance later in the celebration.”

Term by term

2018–2022

Seconded the limestone, unlit Clearspring Greens amendment and voted against the 2020 levy. Voted to deny both street-edge tree waivers and seconded the 2019 denial. Seconded support for the Arts Centre's grant application (2022).

Took part in 5 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Advocated a dedicated traffic officer. Moved the 2024 resolution supporting a regional library in principle. Reported on the first Sikh celebration (2026). Seconded the 2025 outdoor pool grant application and called a park reserve realignment administrative.

Took part in 10 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

Cost-conscious on who pays for local projects, supportive of enforcement on noise and trees, and in his second term a voice for newcomer communities he met in person.
