Quality of life · Council member

Bill Hiebert: quality of life

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about quality-of-life issues in Steinbach (noise, newcomers and inclusion, flags and proclamations, green space, arts and culture) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert said one of the main reasons he ran for council was to see a new recreation centre, and seconded the 2019 event centre partnership. In June 2020, after George Floyd's death, he used his opening remarks to speak about racism, publicly apologized for attitudes he had had to change, and urged everyone to accept all people as equals. No motion followed. In 2022 he raised residents' complaints about engine-brake noise and moved the request for signs on the highways.

He split on the street-edge tree rule, voting to enforce it in 2019 and to waive it in 2020. In 2022–2026 he sat on the regional library working group and seconded the motion ending it, seconded the end of the economic development grant, and voted against the reduced parking for the Friesen Avenue seniors' building.

“Bringing me to the topic of racism, which we need to be aware of and address in our own thinking. … So for that, I want to apologize publicly and say sorry. I want to encourage all of us to accept all people as equals, no matter what color.”

Councillor Bill Hiebert · Opening remarks after George Floyd's death · Jun 16, 2020 · 0:00:15

Term by term

2018–2022

Seconded the event centre partnership. Gave the June 2020 opening remarks on racism, noting Steinbach had over 55 ethnic groups. Voted to deny the 2019 tree waiver and to grant the 2020 one. Voted for the 2020 Clearspring Greens plan and supported the 2021 one, citing safety. Raised engine-brake noise and moved the request for highway signs (2022).

Took part in 7 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Member of the regional library working group; seconded ending council's part in it (2026). Seconded not renewing the economic development grant. Voted against the reduced-parking variance for the Friesen Avenue seniors' building. Seconded the Sports Heritage Committee grant and several tree and dog-park by-law readings.

Took part in 10 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A low-key member on this topic who spoke up on two things residents raised with him (engine-brake noise) and one he raised himself (racism).
