Quality of life · Council member

Chris Goertzen: quality of life

What did Chris Goertzen say and do about quality-of-life issues in Steinbach (noise, newcomers and inclusion, flags and proclamations, green space, arts and culture) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

In short

Mayor Chris Goertzen chaired council during the performing arts centre debate. He objected in April 2016 when council dropped the multiplex and its master plan, then worked to deliver the arts centre approach he said he had not voted for, until outside funding missed the June 30, 2017 deadline and he announced the project would not go ahead in its present form.

He framed the Dutch elm program as a choice to keep the city's elms, praised playground renewals, said the city should be as accessible as possible when he supported a Main Street accessible-parking study, and raised the emerald ash borer threat. He defended the focused grants policy when the Pat Porter seniors' centre asked for help, and voted to table the Arts Centre repair match in June 2018 before supporting it in September.

“Well, we know that we hit June 30th and we did not receive all the funding, and so the project will not move forward in its present form.”

Mayor Chris Goertzen · Announcing the end of the performing arts centre · Jul 4, 2017 · 0:21:01

Term by term

2014–2018

Kept the Dutch elm program and proposed a letter to the minister on the cost shift (2016); objected that dropping the multiplex abandoned the master planning process; worked to deliver the performing arts centre and announced its end in July 2017. Supported the accessible-parking by-law and a Main Street study, asked about the emerald ash borer, defended the grants policy against the seniors' centre request, and supported the Arts Centre match in September 2018 after voting to table it in June.

Took part in 8 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

A planner whose plan was overruled: he championed the recreation master plan, lost it in 2016, and then carried the replacement project to its end.
