Quality of life · Council member

Cari Penner: quality of life

What did Cari Penner say and do about quality-of-life issues in Steinbach (noise, newcomers and inclusion, flags and proclamations, green space, arts and culture) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner served on the 2016 task force on recreation and the arts. In 2018 she seconded the Arts Centre repair match both times, voting against tabling it in June and calling it a golden opportunity to match the group's own grant in September; she said she had advocated for the Arts Council's move into its building.

She moved third reading of the 2017 Accessible Parking By-law, which doubled the fine to $100 and dropped the word 'handicapped,' and moved the application for a provincial grant toward Aquatic Centre repairs in 2018. In 2016 her votes on the arts centre cannot always be told apart from Susan Penner's in the recordings.
