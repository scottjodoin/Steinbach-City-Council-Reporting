Quality of life · Council member

John Fehr: quality of life

What did John Fehr say and do about quality-of-life issues in Steinbach (noise, newcomers and inclusion, flags and proclamations, green space, arts and culture) between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr used a 2016 opening to urge residents to make Steinbach a friendlier and more welcoming city every day. In December 2017, after a resident's letter, he moved that staff examine accessible parking stalls on Main Street, speaking of family members who use wheelchairs; the motion carried over Councillor Zwaagstra's objection, but no report came back in the record.

On the arts, he moved the December 2016 motion that kept the city's $7.5 million share of the performing arts centre with a June 30, 2017 deadline, and stressed downtown parking. In June 2018 he moved to table the Arts Centre repair match, wanting a concrete plan, then supported it in September, saying the city should have kept up its own building.

“We must always look for ways of being a friendlier and more welcoming city every day.”

Councillor John Fehr · Opening remarks · Jul 19, 2016 · 0:00:06
