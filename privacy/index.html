About this site

Privacy

We count how many people read each report. We don't use cookies, we don't track you from site to site, and we never know who you are.

Last updated Nov 9, 2026.

In short

  • We use Cloudflare Web Analytics to count page visits. It sets no cookies, stores nothing on your device, and doesn't fingerprint you.
  • We see totals only, such as how many times the sewer backups report was read in a month. We can't see individual visitors.
  • Why we count: to learn which council topics residents care about, and to show supporters and funders that the site is read, so it can keep going.
  • No ads, and we don't sell anything. We don't sell, rent or share visitor data.

What we count

When a page loads, a small script from Cloudflare Web Analytics records:

  • which page was viewed, and the site or search engine you came from (if any);
  • the general type of browser, operating system and device (for example “Chrome on a phone”);
  • the country the visit came from;
  • how quickly the page loaded, so we can keep pages fast on slow connections.

We see these only as totals in a dashboard. Cloudflare says Web Analytics uses no cookies or local storage and doesn't use your IP address or browser details to identify or follow you. See Cloudflare's privacy policy.

We don't collect your name, email address or exact location, and we don't build a profile of you. Nothing here is used for advertising.

Why readership numbers matter

This site is independent. Turning hundreds of hours of council video into searchable transcripts costs money, and so does keeping reports up to date. Reports are researched with AI assistance and checked against the transcripts and minutes.

Visit counts help in two ways:

  1. Choosing what to cover. If residents read the reports on sewer backups or taxes far more than others, those are the topics to update first, and similar issues are worth covering next.
  2. Justifying funding. People, community groups and grant programs want evidence that their support reaches residents. A count of how many people read the candidate forum pages before the election, for example, shows that the work is used. When we share figures with supporters or funders, they are always totals by page, topic or month, and never anything about an individual reader.

Your choices

Ad blockers and privacy tools often block the counting script. That's fine: every page works exactly the same without it.

Hosting and email

The site is hosted on GitHub Pages. GitHub may log visitors' IP addresses for security, and we can't see those logs. See GitHub's privacy statement.

If you email us, we use your address only to reply, and we never publish it. As on every report, members of the public are identified only by initials.
