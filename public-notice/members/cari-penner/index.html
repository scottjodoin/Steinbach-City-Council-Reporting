Public notice and having a say · Council member

Cari Penner: public hearings and residents' objections

What did Cari Penner say and do when residents objected at public hearings on rezonings, variances and plan changes between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

In 2015–2017 the recordings rarely tell the two Councillors Penner apart, so Cari Penner can be identified with certainty only at the 2016 Quail Ridge garage hearing, where she invited neighbours to report by-law problems, and on the 2016 arts centre survey task force. Other 'Councillor Penner' votes from those years cannot be attributed to her.

In 2018, as Deputy Mayor, she chaired the hearing on 44 townhouses on Maplewood Street and voted to deny them after 49 letters of objection. She voted with the majority to refuse a First Street front-yard variance and to keep parking conditions on a church, and for the 4–3 closing of the Harmony Lane public reserve over two neighbours' objections. She cast the lone votes against a digital sign on Main Street and unpaved parking on Park Road West, neither of which drew objectors.

Term by term

2014–2018

Identified with certainty in 2015–2017 only at Quail Ridge (2016), where she told neighbours to report by-law problems, and on the arts centre survey task force. In 2018 she chaired the Maplewood Street hearing and voted to deny the townhouses, voted to refuse the First Street front-yard variance and to keep the Calvary Chapel parking conditions, voted for the Harmony Lane reserve closing (4–3), and cast lone votes against a digital sign and unpaved parking that nobody objected to.

Took part in 9 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

A record that is mostly unattributable before 2018 and, in 2018, sided with neighbours on Maplewood Street while applying standards strictly to applications no one objected to.
