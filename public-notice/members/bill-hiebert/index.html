Bill Hiebert: public hearings and residents' objections

What did Bill Hiebert say and do when residents objected at public hearings on rezonings, variances and plan changes between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert voted with the majority on nearly every contested hearing in this record, including the 4–3 approval of 32 units on Home Street in 2024. His clearest break was in 2019, when he joined Jac Siemens and Jake Hiebert against the eight-unit building at 44 First Street at second and third reading.

He also voted against the 185 Friesen Avenue parking variance in 2026, was the lone vote against denying an uncontested variance in 2023, and seconded the 2026 denial of 20 units at 330 McKenzie Avenue after 62 letters. The recordings rarely tell the two Councillors Hiebert apart, so we have no remarks of his on this topic that we can quote with confidence.

Term by term

2018–2022

Voted against 44 First Street at second and third reading (2019); otherwise with the majority on every contested hearing, including the Clearspring Greens levy. Thanked objectors at the Wyndham Estate and Ellice Avenue hearings (2021) and played down traffic concerns on First Street.

Took part in 11 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

Voted with the majority on every contested hearing in 2023–2024, including Home Street. Cast the lone vote against denying an uncontested side-yard variance on PTH 52 West (2023). Voted against the 185 Friesen Avenue parking variance (2026), supported Stone Bridge Crossing, and seconded the 330 McKenzie Avenue denial.

Took part in 18 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2022–2026 story.

The trajectory

A quiet majority vote who has broken from it mainly on density on First Street and on parking.
