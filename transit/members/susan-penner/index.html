Susan Penner: transit and accessible transportation

What did Susan Penner say and do about transit and accessible transportation in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor in all three terms covered · Deputy Mayor from November 2026 (topped the 2026 poll)

In short

Susan Penner has no high-confidence quote on transit in the meeting videos. Her main recorded position comes from SteinbachOnline's report of the February 2024 session with the South East Equity Coalition: she appreciated that the coalition agreed a bus loop would not work, and said what was needed was 'the cost analysis, how this would be funded, what grants are available.'

She moved council's acceptance of the new Official Community Plan's recommendations (2024) and its third reading (2025), both routine. The minutes record that she congratulated KodaGo's founder in August 2026. She is Deputy Mayor on the new council.
