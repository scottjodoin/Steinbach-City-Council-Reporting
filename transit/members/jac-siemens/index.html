Jac Siemens: transit and accessible transportation

What did Jac Siemens say and do about transit and accessible transportation in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor in all three terms covered · did not run in 2026

In short

Jac Siemens seconded the December 2021 agreement that handed Handi-Transit to Serving Seniors Inc., calling it a good partnership with a local non-profit that serves seniors. In 2018 he asked Steinbach Taxi what would happen if the city did nothing and how rules would be enforced.

In 2025 he moved first reading of the new Official Community Plan and the Active Transportation Plan. He named 'just starting to understand what public transportation looks like' as one of the plan's themes, and said the city's planning had always asked how to move vehicles, 'not how do we move people outside of vehicles.' He did not propose a transit study or service. He did not run in 2026.

“Our planning has always been in our community, like many communities, is how do we move the vehicles? It's not how do we move people outside of vehicles.”

Councillor Jac Siemens · Moving the Active Transportation Plan · May 6, 2025 · 0:58:35
