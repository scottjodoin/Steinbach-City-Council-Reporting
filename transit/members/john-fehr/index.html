Transit · Council member

John Fehr: transit and accessible transportation

What did John Fehr say and do about transit and accessible transportation in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr moved council's February 2018 application to the province's Small Communities Transportation Fund for half the cost of a new Handi-Transit van, up to $50,000. He called the van 'a very, very important part of the community.' The province turned the application down that August.

The same night, on Steinbach Taxi's request for a by-law, he said the province gave municipalities very little time to react and that more research was needed. He also moved first reading and seconded third reading of the 2018 Official Community Plan (routine).

“It's just the handy van is a very, very important part of the community, and I think it's important that we keep equipment in very good condition, so I think it's a good idea to update it.”

Councillor John Fehr · Moving the Handi-Transit van grant application · Feb 20, 2018 · 0:32:36
