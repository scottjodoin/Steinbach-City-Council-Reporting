Chris Goertzen: transit and accessible transportation

What did Chris Goertzen say and do about transit and accessible transportation in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026?

Mayor until October 2018 (this record covers December 2015 on)

In short

Mayor Chris Goertzen chaired council when the province handed taxi regulation to municipalities in 2018. When Steinbach Taxi asked council for a by-law in February 2018, he said council would ask administration for a small report. In May 2018, SteinbachOnline reported, council adopted administration's recommendation not to create a by-law; that decision was made at a committee meeting that was not recorded.

His council also applied for a provincial grant to replace a Handi-Transit van (the province said no) and heard the province's July 2018 advice to begin planning for a transit system. The Official Community Plan adopted in October 2018 had no transit policy that we found. He made no recorded statement on general transit.

“What we will do, of course, as council, we'll ask administration to give us a small report.”

Mayor Chris Goertzen · After Steinbach Taxi's delegation · Feb 20, 2018 · 1:00:56
