Cari Penner: transit and accessible transportation

What did Cari Penner say and do about transit and accessible transportation in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner seconded council's February 2018 application for a provincial grant to replace a Handi-Transit van (routine; the province said no).

When council decided in May 2018 not to create a taxi by-law, SteinbachOnline reported that she, as Deputy Mayor, said less regulation was best for competition between ride services and taxis. That was a committee meeting, not recorded on video.

Term by term

2014–2018

Seconded the 2018 van grant application (routine). Per SteinbachOnline, backed the May 2018 decision not to regulate taxis, saying less regulation was best for competition.

Took part in 2 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.
