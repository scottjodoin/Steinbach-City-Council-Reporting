Damian Penner: transit and accessible transportation

What did Damian Penner say and do about transit and accessible transportation in Steinbach between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Damian Penner's clearest statement came outside a recorded meeting. SteinbachOnline reported that in February 2024 he said exploring alternatives to a fixed bus loop was 'something that we have an obligation to start looking at exploring.' He seconded the 2024 acceptance of the Official Community Plan's recommendations and moved nothing on transit itself.

He seconded the 2025 Active Transportation Plan. When KodaGo's founder spoke to council in August 2026, a Councillor Penner, most likely Damian Penner, said private innovation 'is often far superior to public spent dollars,' while adding that public transit may be looked at in the future. The recording's speaker labels and the minutes disagree on which Penner spoke, so we do not quote it under his name.
