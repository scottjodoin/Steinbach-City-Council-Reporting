Housing and development · Council member

Cari Penner: housing, apartments, infill and paying for growth

What did Cari Penner say and do about housing and development in Steinbach (affordable housing, apartments and infill, new neighbourhoods, and who pays for growth) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner moved first reading of the Bridge Park seniors' rezoning in 2016 and seconded a one-time servicing exception for a commercial subdivision. Many of her 2016–2017 remarks cannot be told apart from Susan Penner's in the recordings and are not attributed.

As Deputy Mayor from late 2017 she voted for the First Street townhouse rezoning but against its 15-ft setback, arguing infill must fit the whole street, and voted to deny the 44 Maplewood townhouses while saying redevelopment there would renew 'a neighborhood that needs some help'. She moved two of the 2018 Hanover Street house purchases and the withdrawal of the city's objection to the RM of Hanover's development plan.

“For myself, I feel that this is a good thing to do in the fact that it is renewing a neighborhood that needs some help.”

Councillor Cari Penner · On the Maplewood townhouses, which council denied 5–1 · Aug 21, 2018 · 1:02:54

Term by term

2014–2018

She moved first reading of the Bridge Park seniors' rezoning (2016) and seconded the Steinbach North servicing exception. In 2018 she voted for the First Street rezoning but against the 15-ft setback variance (defeated 3–4), voted to deny the Maplewood townhouses (5–1) while saying the developer needed to make the plan fit and ease neighbours' concerns, voted against a parking-surfacing amendment to the new zoning by-law, and moved the 332 and 380 Hanover Street purchases. Her attributions in 2016–2017 are limited because two Councillors Penner served.

Took part in 9 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

Served only in the first term covered. Her few attributed positions favour renewal of older blocks on terms that fit the existing street.
