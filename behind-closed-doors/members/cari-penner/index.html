Behind closed doors · Council member

Cari Penner: decisions made outside public meetings

What did Cari Penner say and do about decisions made outside public meetings (study sessions, committees, closed sessions) and about council's openness between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner seconded the December 2016 motion reaffirming the city's $7.5 million arts centre share after the $24.3 million estimate reached the media. She said that if council's decisions were 'sabotaged and undermined' through the press, it undermined what council had decided.

She voted for the golf club land purchase in 2017, calling it fiscally attractive and citing an appraisal well above the price; opponents said no appraisal had been done, and the question is unresolved in the record. In 2018 she seconded the 2018 budget, and opposed tabling the arts centre matching grant to the study session.

“And if it's sabotaged and undermined and there's misinformation and all manner of manipulation with the press, then it undermines what council has decided.”

Councillor Cari Penner · On the arts centre estimate reaching the media · Dec 20, 2016 · 0:43:05

Term by term

2014–2018

She seconded the arts centre reaffirmation (carried 4–3, 2016) and criticized 'manipulation with the press'. She voted for the golf club land purchase, citing 'the actual appraisal of the land coming in much higher than our offer' (no appraisal was made public), and seconded the 2018 budget. She seconded the arts centre matching grant and voted against tabling it to the study session (2018). In the 2015–2017 recordings the two Councillors Penner are often hard to tell apart, so her remarks are attributed only where certain.

Took part in 6 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

A majority voice on the term's two disputes, who saw the problem in leaks to the media rather than in the closed process; on the arts grant she wanted a decision in public rather than at the study session.
