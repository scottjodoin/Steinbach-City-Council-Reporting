Behind closed doors · Council member

John Fehr: decisions made outside public meetings

What did John Fehr say and do about decisions made outside public meetings (study sessions, committees, closed sessions) and about council's openness between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor 2010–2018 · Deputy Mayor until November 2017

In short

John Fehr moved both of the term's contested decisions: the December 2016 arts centre reaffirmation and the December 2017 golf club land purchase. On the golf deal he said every land negotiation had 'always been behind closed doors' because discussing it first is 'just not prudent'; at the 2018 budget he repeated that 'Land deals are always done in camera' and said it was time to move on.

He also defended the budget process ('We've seen all these numbers before', 2017) and asked to put items on the July study session. He was the member most willing to dissent in public on land: he voted alone against each of the three 2018 downtown lot purchases, explaining that the city had spent more than its land budget.

“Land deals are always done in camera. They're always done behind closed doors. I'm not saying that. You cannot make a land deal in the public eye.”

Councillor John Fehr · Replying to the budget's opponents · Mar 20, 2018 · 0:28:07

Term by term

2014–2018

He moved the arts centre reaffirmation with a sunset date (2016) and the golf club purchase (2017), defending closed negotiation of every land deal. He seconded the mobile-sign by-law that came from SPC (2015), told the 2017 budget hearing council had 'seen all these numbers before', and moved to table the arts centre grant for more information (2018). He voted against the three 2018 downtown land purchases, each brought to a public meeting with its price.

Took part in 9 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2014–2018 story.

The trajectory

He held that negotiation belongs in private and decisions in public, and argued both positions openly; his public votes against downtown land buying show the same council could dissent on the record.
