Behind closed doors · Council member

Bill Hiebert: decisions made outside public meetings

What did Bill Hiebert say and do about decisions made outside public meetings (study sessions, committees, closed sessions) and about council's openness between December 2015 and November 2026?

Councillor since November 2018 · re-elected 2026

In short

Bill Hiebert spoke rarely on how council does its business. In January 2019 he was one of three members who voted to send the purchasing by-law to the Strategic Priorities Committee (defeated 3–4). He seconded the event centre agreement approved on the night its package arrived (2019) and voted with the majority on the Clearspring Greens levy.

In 2025 he seconded the public decision not to renew the economic development office grant, speaking about its overlap with the Chamber of Commerce. In 2026 he moved third reading of the event centre loan and the golf club's donation receipts, a request the mayor welcomed for leaving 'a trail of paper'. Re-elected in 2026, he was reappointed to the watershed district board, whose own minutes record motions to go in and out of camera.

Term by term

2018–2022

He voted for Jake Hiebert's motion to send the purchasing by-law to SPC (2019). He seconded the event centre memorandum of understanding and grant application, approved unanimously on a package council received that evening (2019), and seconded the honorary street naming policy that staff said had seen 'much debate' at SPC (2022).

Took part in 7 of the items in this report during this term. Read the 2018–2022 story.

2022–2026

He voted with the majority for 5% development fees (2023), seconded the non-renewal of the economic development office grant (2025), and moved third reading of the event centre loan and the golf club donation receipts (2026). No high-confidence quote from him on this topic was found.
