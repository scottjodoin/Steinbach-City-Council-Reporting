Planning for the future · Council member

Cari Penner: planning for the next 20 years

What did Cari Penner say and do about Steinbach's long-term planning (growth, land, water and wastewater, neighbours, long-range capital and climate) between December 2015 and November 2026, and how did that change over time?

Councillor 2010–2018 (and an earlier term before 2010) · Deputy Mayor from November 2017

In short

Cari Penner served on the 2016 task force that surveyed residents on recreation facilities. She seconded the 4–3 motion reaffirming the arts centre, saying it was dysfunctional to keep undoing a council decision once made. She also seconded and argued for a one-time relaxation of servicing standards for the Steinbach North subdivision (2016).

As Deputy Mayor from November 2017 she moved two of the 2018 Hanover Street land purchases. She argued that long-term vision needs several councils to follow the vision of earlier ones. She moved the 2018 objection to La Broquerie's plan, and the withdrawal of the Hanover objection once Hanover changed its plan. She voted against relaxing hard-surfacing rules in the new zoning by-law. Her vote on the golf club land deal could not be confirmed.

“I believe that it's up to council to have long-term vision. And sometimes long-term vision requires several different councils following the vision of the previous councils, and that is a good thing because otherwise there would be no continuity in our city planning.”

Councillor Cari Penner · On a downtown land purchase · Oct 16, 2018 · 0:32:03
