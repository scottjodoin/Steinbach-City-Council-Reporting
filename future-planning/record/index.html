Every decision and debate on planning for the future

What has Steinbach City Council decided, debated and promised about the city’s long-term future since December 2015, and how did each item end?

In short

Each row is one decision, debate, promise or request on the topic, from the minutes and the meeting videos. Filter by term, year, theme, council member or result. “No public result” means we found no public decision or follow-up; much planning work happens in closed study sessions and committees.
